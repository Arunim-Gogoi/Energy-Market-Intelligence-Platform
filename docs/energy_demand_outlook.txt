Global Energy Demand Outlook

Total primary energy demand continues to grow, driven mainly by population growth and rising
living standards in emerging economies, particularly in South and Southeast Asia and parts
of Africa. Electricity demand is growing faster than overall energy demand due to
electrification of transport, heating, and industrial processes, along with rising demand
from data centers and artificial intelligence workloads.

Natural gas is often positioned as a transition fuel, offering lower emissions than coal for
power generation while providing dispatchable capacity to complement variable renewables.
However, gas demand growth varies significantly by region, with strong growth in Asian LNG
importing markets contrasted against flat or declining demand in parts of Europe following
efficiency measures and accelerated renewable deployment after supply disruptions.

Industrial energy demand, particularly from steel, cement, and chemicals, remains difficult
to decarbonize and is a focus area for emerging technologies such as green hydrogen, carbon
capture, and electrification of high-temperature heat processes.
